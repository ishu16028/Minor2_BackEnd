Resume Tips

  • Use clear section headings (EDUCATION, EXPERIENCE, SKILLS, etc.)
  • Avoid complex tables and layouts
  • Prefer standard fonts like Arial, Calibri, or Times New Roman
  • Include your full name at the top
  • Make sure your email and phone number are clearly visible

  • Use action verbs to describe your achievements
  • Include quantifiable results when possible
  • List relevant skills prominently
  • Customize for each job application
  • Keep it concise (1-2 pages maximum)

  • Spelling and grammar errors
  • Using generic objectives
  • Including irrelevant personal information
  • Using an unprofessional email address
  • Overcrowding the page with text
